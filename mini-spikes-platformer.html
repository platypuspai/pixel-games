MINI PLATFORMER: SPIKES

💡 What is it?
A multi-level spike challenge. Avoid the purple spikes, navigate through obstacles, and reach the yellow exit portal!

🎮 Controls:
🏃‍♂️ Move Left/Right: Use Left/Right Arrow Keys.
🦘 Jump: Up Arrow Key.
💀 Spikes: Touching the purple spikes will instantly reset you to the start of the level.
